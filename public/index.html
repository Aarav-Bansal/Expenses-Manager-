Connect a Google Sheet

Your sheet needs the header columns Merchant, Amount, Category and Status. Rows with a blank Status become demons. Optional: Date, Note, Reviewed At.

How to play

  • ←→ or AD move the exorcist
  • 123 pick a spell: Work, Personal, Unknown
  • Space hold to cast. The first spell to hit decides a demon's category
  • Bigger demons take more hits and fall slower
  • A demon that crosses the Ledger Line costs Sanity, then returns once
  • Omens glow near the line. Follow them for bonus points, or trust your own judgement
  • P pause. Touch screens get on-screen buttons
